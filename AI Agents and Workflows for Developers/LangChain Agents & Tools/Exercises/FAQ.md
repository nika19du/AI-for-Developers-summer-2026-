# Company FAQ

## Q1. What is the warranty?

The warranty period is **24 months for individuals** and **12 months for legal entities**.

The warranty does not cover mechanical damage, liquid damage, improper use, or unauthorized repairs.

---

## Q2. How do I make a warranty claim?

You can submit a warranty claim through the form on our website or by contacting customer support.

Please provide:

- your order number;
- a short description of the problem;
- photos of the product, if relevant.

Our service department will review the request and respond within **2 business days**.

---

## Q3. How long does a repair take?

Warranty repairs may take up to **30 calendar days**.

If a required spare part is unavailable, customer support will contact you with the available options.

---

## Q4. Can I return an order?

Yes. You can return an order within **14 days of receiving it**, provided that the product and packaging meet the return conditions.

To request a return, contact customer support and provide your order number.

---

## Q5. What are the delivery times?

Typical delivery times are:

- **Sofia:** within 24 hours for eligible orders placed before 4:00 PM;
- **Other locations:** usually within 1–3 business days.

Delivery times may vary depending on product availability, courier workload, weekends, and public holidays.

Free delivery is available for orders over **BGN 150**.

---

## Q6. What payment methods do you accept?

We accept:

- cash on delivery;
- bank transfer;
- card payment.

Business customers may also be eligible for deferred payment, subject to an approved credit limit and agreed payment terms.

---

## Q7. When will I receive my invoice?

The invoice is issued after the order has been processed and is provided electronically or together with the order, depending on the selected delivery method.

If you need a copy of an invoice, contact customer support and provide your order number.

---

## Q8. How can I check my order status?

Provide your **order number** to customer support.

The order status can be checked in the order database and may include:

- **Processing** — the order is being prepared;
- **Ready for shipment** — the order is prepared and waiting for the courier;
- **Shipped** — the order has left the warehouse;
- **Delivered** — the order has been delivered;
- **Cancelled** — the order has been cancelled.

---

## Q9. Can I cancel my order?

An order can normally be cancelled if it has **not yet been shipped**.

Please provide your order number so that customer support can check its current status before confirming the cancellation.

---

## Q10. My order is delayed. What should I do?

Contact customer support and provide your order number.

The support team will check the latest order status and available delivery information before responding.

---

## Q11. Can I change my delivery address?

The delivery address may be changed while the order is still being processed.

If the order has already been handed over to the courier, changing the address may not be possible or may require contacting the courier directly.

---

## Q12. How should customer support handle questions?

Customer support should always use the available company information and current order data.

For example, when a customer asks:

> "Where is my order and can I cancel it?"

the support system should:

1. Check the **order status** in the database.
2. Check the relevant **company policy** in this FAQ.
3. Combine the information.
4. Give the customer one clear and helpful response.

The assistant should **never invent an order status, company policy, delivery date, or refund decision** when the information is unavailable.
